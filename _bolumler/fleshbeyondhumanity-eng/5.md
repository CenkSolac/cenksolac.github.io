  A rustling sound came from the wind brushing against the leaves.\r

  I was watching the sun rise with my eyes open.\r

  This was my third day in this damned world, and I had finally accepted that this world was real.\r

  I was trapped in this beautiful environment, watching the scenery. Though I couldn't really call it scenery, since the trees in front of me blocked my view. Still, it was pleasant, and I could see the sun.\r

  But now I had to get down.\r

  When I looked down and saw how far I was from the ground, I trembled considerably.\r

  I was far too high.\r

  Even though I wasn't afraid of heights, climbing down without any safety equipment felt incredibly nerve-racking.\r

  Still, I had to go down.\r

  I decided to try descending the way a mountaineer would.\r

  I slowly made my way downward, digging my claws in one after another.\r

  This idea worked.\r

  Every time I dug one of my claws in, I got closer to the ground.\r

  Before long, I realized that something was wrong.\r

  My vision was becoming blurry.\r

  But I couldn't stop anymore. I was already halfway down.\r

  No matter what happened, I decided to reach the ground.\r

  I kept watching my movements as I descended.\r

  Every movement of my claws pierced a small part of the tree.\r

  As I climbed down, I looked at the tiny holes my claws had made.\r

  Along with this, a sudden exhaustion came over me.\r

  Even though I couldn't understand why, I continued descending.\r

  Eventually, I reached the ground.\r

  I had never brought my true thoughts to the surface. Even when I didn't want to, I had always tried to think positively and keep myself optimistic.\r

  Screaming inside, I roared, \"I can't take it anymore!\"\r

  No matter how hard I tried to psych myself up, I was a modern human. I was someone who could talk whenever I wanted, socialize from time to time, and had plenty of money and possessions. I had a house, a car, and plenty of friends.\r

  I had never lived in a place this difficult in my entire life.\r

  I had no one, and I was on the verge of falling into despair. I hadn't encountered even a single human being. On top of that, I was all alone in a place unlike anything I had ever known.\r

  Even the filthy air humans had polluted was gone.\r

  And this was incredibly demoralizing.\r

  The race of creatures I had thought was intelligent was still in a primitive age. They had nothing.\r

  I couldn't stop thinking, What if the human race doesn't exist?\r

  Because of that, I had to enter the ruins. I wouldn't give up.\r

  The ruins were made entirely of plain stone.\r

  I got down on all fours and started running.\r

  I wanted to see everything that was inside the ruins. I needed a piece of hope.\r

  So I ran with all my strength.\r

  Every step I took made the sound of claws against the ground.\r

  My running was fast, furious, and desperate.\r

  The flesh of my hands and feet hitting the ground was tense.\r

  When I finally stopped, the ruins stood in front of me.\r

  My rib cage was vibrating, and every vibration produced an angry sound.\r

  It was a sound that would startle the entire forest.\r

  This was the battle cry of a predatory monster.\r

  The lookouts assigned to watch over the tribe noticed me and stared at me in fear.\r

  Without wasting any time, I threw myself toward my death.\r

  I burst through the main entrance.\r

  The scorpions that were preparing to leave saw me and stared at me in astonishment.\r

  With a sudden swipe of my claw, I tore one of them apart.\r

  Some of them recovered from their shock and rushed at me with lightning-fast strikes.\r

  As I launched my hands forward, I tore two scorpions apart.\r

  With the lookouts arriving as well, I was completely surrounded.\r

  My situation was very bad.\r

  The scorpions that had climbed onto the walls and were preparing to jump on me had only one desire: to kill me.\r

  The moment all the scorpions jumped at me, time seemed to slow down.\r

  I was exhausted by my own thoughts. I didn't even want to think anymore.\r

  I let myself go and allowed them to cut me.\r

  \"CLINK.\"\r

  With that sound, incredible pain surged throughout my entire body.\r

  My flesh was being cut every second. The pain was indescribable.\r

  I was screaming in agony.\r

  I tried to push them away, but my body wouldn't obey me.\r

  Those red eyes I could see were about to take my life.\r

  In the end, my body, lying on the ground, slowly grew cold as it was torn apart.\r

  My vision slowly began to turn black.\r

  I wasn't made for this world...\r

  As soon as I opened my eyes, I saw the scorpion on the ceiling.\r

  Its body was impaled in the wall. Small drops of blood were dripping down from its body.\r

  When I looked around, I saw holes in the walls and many dead scorpions on the ground.\r

  My plan had worked.\r

  I turned my head with a sad expression and thought to myself, I was simply much smarter than all of you.\r

  It had been carved into the wall.\r

  My hope had begun to grow again.\r

  I even remembered some details I had forgotten when I saw it. I had seen a rabbit and a hedgehog.\r

  I laughed and said, \"How could I have forgotten this?\"\r

  When I looked at the writing again, my right arm suddenly moved on its own and lunged toward it. The writing was completely shattered, as if it had turned to dust. I just stared blankly, unable to make any sense of what had happened.\r

  After that, my arm returned to its place as if nothing had happened.\r

  With my mouth hanging open, I said, \"Hey, this has never happened before.\""
