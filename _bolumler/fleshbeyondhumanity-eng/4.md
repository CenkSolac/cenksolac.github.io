metin: "I saw two monsters in front of me. They were quite short.\r

  They were about one meter tall. They had eight legs, three pincers, and one mouth.\r

  They looked somewhat like scorpions.\r

  Their most distinctive feature was the middle pincer, which protruded from the upper-middle part of their bodies and looked like a mouth.\r

  As I secretly watched them, they were forming some kind of sentences that I couldn't understand.\r

  One of them was moving from side to side with its body while the other watched. Its movements were sudden and fast.\r

  After moving around for a while, it stopped and started walking. The other one then made a gesture as if telling it to move, and they began leaving the area.\r

  I started following them.\r

  This was the first time since coming to this world that I had seen a monster that could talk.\r

  I was excited.\r

  I secretly followed them from only ten meters away.\r

  As they continued walking, I tried to analyze what they were saying. They were talking, but it seemed like they kept repeating the same sentences.\r

  While they were walking, they stopped in front of a tree.\r

  The monster that had been moving as if it were dancing earlier brought its mouth-like pincer toward the tree, picked a blue fruit, and gave it to the other one. Then it picked one for itself as well.\r

  They brought the fruits to their mouths, ate them happily, and continued on their way.\r

  This made me even more certain.\r

  They might have a home and their own language.\r

  Eventually, we arrived somewhere.\r

  What I saw was a large ruin surrounded by trees.\r

  The ruin, ringed with stones, looked old and badly damaged.\r

  Its walls had collapsed, and the stones were broken apart.\r

  The two monsters quietly continued inside.\r

  I wanted to follow them, but I didn't dare because I thought it might be dangerous. Besides, I had no idea how many monsters were inside.\r

  I was about to decide to wait when suddenly—\r

  A sound rose from behind me.\r

  \"KWEEEKK!\"\r

  The moment I heard the scream, I turned around.\r

  It was one of the same kind of monsters, and it was screaming like it had gone insane.\r

  Its voice echoed through the forest, almost like a cry for help.\r

  Normally, that would have scared me, but I was strangely calm.\r

  The others were coming out of the ruin.\r

  One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten...\r

  Their numbers were increasing rapidly.\r

  To stop it from screaming anymore, I lunged at it.\r

  As soon as I got close, it swung its right pincer at me. I blocked it with my hand in an instant, then turned my hand into a spear and launched it toward its small head like a harpoon.\r

  My hand shot forward at incredible speed.\r

  \"CRACK!\"\r

  The sound came at the same moment.\r

  It died instantly.\r

  But it was already too late.\r

  My position had been revealed, and I was now in their sight. The scorpion-like monsters that saw me flinched and stared at me in shock.\r

  Around twenty or thirty of them were watching me.\r

  They had seen me destroy their friend with a single movement of my hand.\r

  They stared at me in shock.\r

  I didn't know if it was because they had probably never seen a monster like me before, but they didn't make a move.\r

  Looking at their eyes, I could feel that they were afraid.\r

  Knowing that they might follow me if I didn't scare them enough, I roared with all my strength.\r

  \"HAAAARGGG!!!!\"\r

  The group suddenly flinched and pulled back. Some of them stepped forward, probably warriors who wanted to protect their own family in this dangerous situation. But even they hesitated.\r

  When I saw that they had no desire to fight, I didn't stay any longer. I took the corpse I had killed and walked away.\r

  I looked behind me, and when I saw that nobody was following me, I let out a deep breath.\r

  This was the outcome I wanted the least.\r

  I had made an entire tribe my enemy.\r

  I could no longer communicate with them normally.\r

  I needed a new plan.\r

  First, let's think.\r

  I had to enter that ruin.\r

  It couldn't have been built by those creatures. It must have been made by something else.\r

  Maybe humans. Or some other creatures.\r

  I didn't know.\r

  But the one thing I was certain of was that there could be a clue inside.\r

  As I got lost in my thoughts, I became hungry.\r

  When I looked at the monster I had killed, I couldn't suppress my hunger and started eating it.\r

  ...\r

  After I finished eating, I realized that there was a part of its body where the shell wasn't hard, and I could break it easily with my hand.\r

  These creatures were probably primitive. They didn't have any tools or anything else.\r

  That reduced what they could teach me.\r

  They might have shown human-like behavior, but they weren't human.\r

  As disappointment filled me, a spark of anger appeared inside me.\r

  I had made my decision.\r

  I would kill them all.\r

  The sky was brighter now.\r

  I had plenty of time.\r

  Since I couldn't attack all of them at once, I needed to reduce their numbers.\r

  My plan was simple.\r

  I would kill the scorpion-like creatures that left the ruin to search for food. That way, I could slowly reduce their numbers.\r

  I moved into action and approached the area where I had been before.\r

  There was nobody outside.\r

  I circled around the ruin and carefully checked the surroundings. I didn't want to be caught from behind like last time.\r

  After making sure there was no one around, I decided to wait.\r

  After a while, seven scorpions came out of the ruin.\r

  They were moving together.\r

  They were heading in one direction, so I took the opportunity and started following them.\r

  They were silent.\r

  None of them made a sound. They simply walked.\r

  After walking for twelve minutes, an opportunity finally presented itself.\r

  They were gathering fruit from a tree.\r

  This was the moment their guard was at its loosest. One of them had moved away from the group.\r

  I followed it closely.\r

  The scorpion had no idea I was there.\r

  I picked up a fist-sized stone from the ground.\r

  I moved closer.\r

  I was very close now. About six meters, I estimated.\r

  Its back was turned toward me, so I couldn't kill it instantly.\r

  I made a soft sound, like whispering in its ear, so it would turn around. Then I hurled the stone.\r

  Before it could understand anything, its face was blown apart.\r

  The first one was down.\r

  I quietly returned to where the group was.\r

  Three of them were gathering fruit while the other three were watching the surroundings.\r

  From here on, it was time to fight.\r

  I wanted to use my hand like a spear and kill one instantly, but because of their flexible bodies, there was a good chance I would miss.\r

  I picked one of the fruit gatherers as my target.\r

  Its attention was completely focused on the fruit.\r

  Taking advantage of that, I stretched my hand along the ground and moved it toward the creature like a tiny arm crawling through the grass.\r

  It was very difficult to notice.\r

  The moment I caught its leg, I pulled my arm back at full speed.\r

  \"KWEEEKK!\"\r

  It screamed.\r

  The three guards immediately moved at the same time and ran toward the source of the sound.\r

  They had noticed me.\r

  But it was already too late.\r

  Before they could engage me, I turned my left hand into a spear and pierced the chest of the one I had caught with a single strike.\r

  The ones coming after me stopped for a moment.\r

  They remembered me.\r

  I stood on two legs and roared.\r

  All three of them were afraid.\r

  They understood that if they ran, they would die one by one.\r

  Even though this situation annoyed me, I had to kill them.\r

  The first one lunged at me, aiming its three pincers at my neck.\r

  I dodged with a quick maneuver.\r

  This time, one attacked from below while another attacked from above.\r

  Knowing that I would be hit by one of them no matter what, I tried to pierce the one in the air with my right hand.\r

  But it grabbed onto a tree with its long pincer and pulled itself backward.\r

  I missed.\r

  \"KIEEWW!\"\r

  The one attacking from below went for my leg.\r

  I couldn't dodge it.\r

  The moment its pincer pierced my leg, pain spread through it.\r

  I quickly attacked with my hand.\r

  Somehow, it dodged again.\r

  The three of them surrounded me and stared at me.\r

  Then they all jumped at me at the same time.\r

  I was at a disadvantage.\r

  They were so fast that I couldn't keep up with them.\r

  They struck me again and again.\r

  Even though I had overwhelming strength, it meant nothing if I couldn't keep up with them.\r

  So I let them attack me.\r

  The sensation of their attacks piercing my body was incredibly painful.\r

  My body was being cut apart, like scissors cutting through paper.\r

  I let the attack aimed at my neck get closer.\r

  At the last moment, I grabbed its long pincer with my hand and slammed it into the ground at incredible speed.\r

  I extended my other hand.\r

  \"SHLUK!\"\r

  I pierced its body.\r

  It died instantly.\r

  Two guards remained.\r

  The ones still attacking kept going without even understanding what had happened.\r

  The pain was becoming unbearable.\r

  It was driving me insane.\r

  With fewer of them left, I suddenly lunged at one of them.\r

  Despite how fast it was, I somehow managed to hit it.\r

  The impact sent it staggering to the side.\r

  I immediately lunged at the other one.\r

  Before it could understand what was happening, I jumped on top of it and pierced its head with my claw.\r

  I turned my head at incredible speed and looked at the one that had been thrown to the ground.\r

  It was trying to escape with its heavily injured body.\r

  But it was useless.\r

  I jumped on top of it, opened my mouth wide, and tore it apart.\r

  An incredible hunger suddenly overwhelmed me, and I immediately ate the three guards I had killed.\r

  The two fruit gatherers had escaped.\r

  This wasn't the outcome I wanted, but there was nothing I could do about it.\r

  The pain disappeared shortly afterward.\r

  Thanks to my body's rapid healing, it had already returned to its previous state.\r

  I took a deep breath.\r

  This battle had exhausted me.\r

  I decided to return to the hollow and rest.\r

  Fortunately, I had broken tree branches along the way so I wouldn't lose my path.\r

  I started moving with the sun behind me.\r

  After about three hours, I realized I was lost.\r

  Even though I had found the ruin, I couldn't find the hollow no matter what I did.\r

  It was getting dark.\r

  I urgently needed to find somewhere to take shelter.\r

  Two more hours passed.\r

  It was completely dark.\r

  Since I had fallen asleep immediately on the first day, I hadn't had time to test my vision at night.\r

  But I could still see up to a certain distance.\r

  I couldn't walk any farther.\r

  I still didn't know how dangerous the night was, so I decided to attempt something I had never tried before.\r

  There were both large and small trees in this forest.\r

  I picked out a large tree and decided to climb it.\r

  The moment I grabbed onto the tree with my claws, I was firmly attached to it.\r

  That was good news.\r

  I slowly began climbing the tree, taking careful steps.\r

  I had already climbed three or four meters.\r

  I couldn't even look down.\r

  After climbing farther, I reached six or seven meters.\r

  I was halfway up.\r

  Shouts of joy were rising inside me, but that didn't last long.\r

  My exhausted muscles were screaming from the strain, and fatigue began overwhelming me.\r

  I barely reached the top.\r

  Fortunately, three large branches extended outward in a triangular shape, creating a safe enough area where I could lie down.\r

  I quickly threw myself onto it, took a deep breath, and looked at the moon.\r

  When I looked at the moon, I noticed it wasn't alone. Beside the white moon hung a second one, and it was blue.\r

  But I was too tired to care.\r

  I quickly surrendered myself to sleep.\r

  ...\r

  These two days had been enough to drive me insane.\r

  I was on the verge of losing my mind.\r

  While all of this was happening, I kept torturing myself with one thought:\r

  What if there were no humans at all?\r

  What if I was completely alone?"
